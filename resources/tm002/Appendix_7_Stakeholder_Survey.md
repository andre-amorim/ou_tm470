**Target Audience:** Computer Science Students, FOSS Developers, and System Administrators.

*   **Anonymity:** No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected.
*   **Data Use:** Responses will be analyzed in aggregate to inform the design of the "nixB" Didactic Laboratory.
*   **Withdrawal:** You may stop the survey at any time before submission.
*   **Consent:** By proceeding, you consent to the use of your anonymized data for academic research.
