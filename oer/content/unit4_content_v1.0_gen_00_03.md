# 4.3 Sovereignty and Sustainability



## Economic Reproducibility


Technical reproducibility (Nix) and economic sustainability (Bitcoin) are two sides of the same sovereign coin. As Nadia Eghbal noted in <!--UnknownTag: Emphasis :UnknownTag--> (2016), open-source infrastructure is often underfunded and overused.

By integrating the <!--UnknownTag: Shareders :UnknownTag--> into our LNbits ecosystem, we demonstrate how value can flow directly to the maintainers of the tools we depend on. This is "Economic Reproducibility"—ensuring the human infrastructure is as resilient as the digital one.

In our lab, the Scrum extension is not just a tool for project management; it is a gateway to a circular economy where developers are compensated in sats for their modular contributions.
