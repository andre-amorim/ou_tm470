# 🎓 Pedagogical Framework: An Object-Oriented Approach to nixB

## 🏛️ Theoretical Foundation
This course utilizes the **Object-Oriented (OO) Educational Design** model proposed by **Bill Tait (1997)**. By treating educational content and process as encapsulated, modular objects, `nixB` achieves a "Didactic Laboratory" environment that mirrors the software engineering principles it teaches.

## 🔗 Mapping the Tait OO Framework to nixB
Following Tait's methodology, the `nixB` course is structured into three primary "Educational Classes":

### 1. The Presentation Class (Units 1 & 2)
*   **Tait Definition:** Summarizes the teaching functions (orientating, motivating, clarifying).
*   **nixB Implementation:**
    *   **Unit 1 (The Gap):** Focuses on "Concept Formation" (Tait, 1997, p. 171) regarding the Reproducibility Gap.
    *   **Unit 2 (Nix & uv):** Provides the "Hierarchical Structure" of the Nix mental model, moving from imperative to declarative states.
*   **Goal:** Establish the declarative blueprint before moving to practice.

### 2. The Practice Class (Unit 3)
*   **Tait Definition:** Represents the learning functions and the "Experiential Learning Cycle" (Practice/Reflection).
*   **nixB Implementation:**
    *   **The Bitcoin/LNbits Laboratory:** A self-contained, modular object where the learner interacts with a high-stakes technical stack.
    *   **Reflective Practice:** Students are not just following a script but are "Orchestrating a Hermetic Stack," requiring them to reflect on dependency relationships.
*   **Goal:** Facilitate "Learning as Doing" through reproducible infrastructure.

### 3. The Assessment Class (Unit 4)
*   **Tait Definition:** Represents management functions, primarily quality assurance and verification.
*   **nixB Implementation:**
    *   **The Verification Loop:** Uses Nix's "Bit-Identical" properties to provide automated feedback.
    *   **Self-Correction:** The system proves its own fidelity, allowing the learner to verify their reproduction against the source of truth.
*   **Goal:** Close the loop with mathematical certainty.

## 🚀 Didactic Innovation
By adopting Tait’s "Student-Centred, Open Ended Structure" (p. 171), `nixB` allows learners to treat units as **independent, replaceable objects**. This modularity ensures that the course itself is as reproducible and maintainable as the software environments it describes.
