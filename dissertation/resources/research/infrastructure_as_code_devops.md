# Research: Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and DevOps

> Source: Wikipedia (Reference via USR)
> Date: 2026-04-14

IaC can be a key attribute of enabling best practices in DevOps. Developers become more involved in defining configuration and Ops teams get involved earlier in the development process. Tools that utilize IaC bring visibility to the state and configuration of servers and ultimately provide the visibility to users within the enterprise, aiming to bring teams together to maximize their efforts. 

Automation in general aims to take the confusion and error-prone aspect of manual processes and make it more efficient, and productive. Allowing for better software and applications to be created with flexibility, less downtime, and an overall cost-effective way for the company. IaC is intended to reduce the complexity that kills efficiency out of manual configuration. Automation and collaboration are considered central points in DevOps; infrastructure automation tools are often included as components of a DevOps toolchain.

## Key Takeaways for nixb (TM470)
- **Collaboration:** By using Nix as IaC, the 'developer' (the user) and the 'ops' (the reproducible environment) are unified.
- **Efficiency:** Reducing the 'complexity that kills efficiency' through deterministic Nix flakes.
- **Visibility:** Providing clear visibility into the system configuration, directly addressing TM353 concerns regarding configuration drift.
